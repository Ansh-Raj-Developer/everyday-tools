About Everyday Tools

Simple tools for everyday calculations and tasks.

What is Everyday Tools?

Everyday Tools is a collection of free online tools designed to make common calculations and everyday tasks faster and easier.

Instead of installing separate applications for simple calculations, you can use our browser-based tools directly from your phone, tablet or computer.

Our Goal

Our goal is to create simple, accessible and useful tools that provide clear results without unnecessary complexity.

What We Offer

Everyday Tools includes calculators and utility tools for areas such as finance, education, mathematics, text processing and everyday conversions.

Free to Use

Our tools are designed to be freely accessible. You can use them whenever you need them without installing additional software.
