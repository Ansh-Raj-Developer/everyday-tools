Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Information We Collect

Everyday Tools is designed to provide online calculators and utility tools. Many tools perform calculations directly in your browser and do not require you to create an account.

If you contact us by email, we may receive information that you voluntarily provide, such as your email address and the contents of your message.

How We Use Information

Information voluntarily provided to us may be used to respond to questions, suggestions, feedback or reports about the website.

Cookies and Advertising

Everyday Tools may use third-party services in the future, including analytics or advertising services. These services may use cookies or similar technologies to provide their services, measure website usage or display relevant advertisements.

Third-Party Services

If third-party services are added to Everyday Tools, those services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

Calculator Data

Information entered into a calculator is generally processed within your browser. However, you should avoid entering sensitive personal, financial or confidential information into any online tool unless the page specifically states how that information is handled.

Children's Privacy

Everyday Tools does not knowingly request personal information from children through account registration. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that we can review the matter.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, features or third-party services change. Any updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page.
