What Is a Percentage?

A percentage is a way of expressing a number as a fraction of 100. Percentages are useful for calculating marks, discounts, price changes and many everyday mathematical problems.

Percentage Formula

Percentage = (Part / Total) × 100

For example, if you score 80 marks out of 100, your percentage is 80%.

Percentage Calculation Examples

Example 1: Finding a Percentage

What is 20% of 500?

  • Percentage: 20%
  • Number: 500
  • Result: 100

Example 2: Finding Percentage Change

If a product's price increases from ₹800 to ₹1,000, the percentage increase is 25%.

  • Original value: ₹800
  • New value: ₹1,000
  • Percentage increase: 25%

Understanding Percentage Results

A percentage expresses a value as a fraction of 100. Percentage change measures how much a value has increased or decreased relative to its original value.

A positive percentage change indicates an increase, while a negative percentage change indicates a decrease.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide the result by 100. For example, 20% of 500 is 100.

How are percentage increases calculated?

Subtract the original value from the new value, divide the difference by the original value and multiply by 100.

Can percentages exceed 100%?

Yes. A percentage can exceed 100% when the value is greater than the reference amount.
