What is an EMI Calculator?

An EMI calculator helps you estimate the fixed monthly payment required to repay a loan over a specified period.

How is EMI calculated?

EMI is calculated using the loan amount, monthly interest rate and total number of monthly payments.

EMI Calculation Example

Suppose you borrow ₹1,00,000 at an annual interest rate of 12% for 1 year.

  • Loan amount: ₹1,00,000
  • Annual interest rate: 12%
  • Loan tenure: 12 months
  • Monthly EMI: ₹8,884.88
  • Total payment: ₹1,06,618.54
  • Total interest: ₹6,618.54

This example uses a fixed interest rate and monthly reducing-balance calculations.

Important Note

This calculator provides an estimate. Actual loan payments may vary due to processing fees, insurance, changes in interest rates and lender-specific terms.

How Does an EMI Calculator Work?

An EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is the fixed monthly payment made towards repaying a loan. It includes both principal and interest. The EMI depends on the loan amount, interest rate and loan tenure.

EMI Formula

EMI = P × R × (1 + R)^N / ((1 + R)^N − 1)

P is the principal loan amount, R is the monthly interest rate expressed as a decimal, and N is the total number of monthly instalments.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does EMI mean?

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Instalment. It is the amount paid every month to repay a loan.

Does a longer loan tenure reduce EMI?

Generally, a longer tenure reduces the monthly EMI but increases the total interest paid, assuming the same interest rate.
