What is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator determines the time elapsed between a person's date of birth and a selected date.

How does it work?

The calculator compares the year, month and day of the two dates to determine the person's age. It accounts for different month lengths and leap years.

What Is an Age Calculator?

An Age Calculator calculates the time between your date of birth and a selected date. It can express your age in years, months and days.

How Is Age Calculated?

Your age is calculated by comparing your date of birth with the current or selected date. The calculation accounts for different month lengths and leap years.

How to Use the Age Calculator

  1. Enter your date of birth.
  2. Select the comparison date, if available.
  3. Click the Calculate button.
  4. View your age in years, months and days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I calculate my exact age in days?

Yes. An age calculator can determine the number of days between your date of birth and the selected date.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

A correctly implemented age calculator accounts for leap years and the varying lengths of calendar months.

Can I calculate someone else's age?

Yes. Enter their date of birth to calculate their age.
