What Is a Discount?

A discount is a reduction in the original price of a product or service. Discounts are usually expressed as percentages and help customers calculate their savings.

Discount Formula

Discount Amount = (Original Price × Discount Percentage) / 100

Final Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example of a Discount

If a product costs ₹1,000 and has a 20% discount, you save ₹200. The final price is ₹800.

Discount Calculation Examples

Example 1: Finding a Discount

Suppose a shirt costs ₹1,000 and has a 20% discount.

  • Original price: ₹1,000
  • Discount: 20%
  • Money saved: ₹200
  • Final price: ₹800

Example 2: Comparing Discounts

Consider a product priced at ₹2,000.

  • At 10% off, you save ₹200.
  • At 25% off, you save ₹500.
  • The final prices are ₹1,800 and ₹1,500, respectively.

How Discounts Work

A discount reduces the original price of a product by a specified percentage or amount. The final price is the original price minus the discount.

Always check whether taxes, delivery charges or other fees are included in the displayed price.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a discount?

Multiply the original price by the discount percentage and divide by 100 to find the discount amount.

How do I calculate the final sale price?

Subtract the discount amount from the original price to get the final sale price.

Can I calculate a 100% discount?

Yes. A 100% discount reduces the price to zero, assuming there are no additional charges.
